FAQ

Built for memories that matter.

What is Memories Unlocked?

A digital legacy and travel-memory experience for recording journeys, meaningful locations, stories and clues that loved ones can follow later.

Can I add past journeys?

Yes. The product is being designed to let you backdate journeys and preserve memories from places you have already visited.

Can journeys be private?

Privacy and family sharing are part of the product roadmap, with private, shared and legacy journeys planned.
