HELP & SUPPORT

Keep your story moving.

Practical help for signing in, saving journeys and memories, adding photographs and keeping your private collection connected.

Start with your account

Open the app at app.memoriesunlocked.app. Sign in to your Memories Unlocked account before adding important journeys or photographs so your content can be saved to your private cloud account.

Important: if the app tells you that a journey is saved on this device or that cloud saving is unavailable, do not assume it has synced. Keep the page open, check your connection, sign in again if needed and confirm the app shows a cloud-connected state before continuing.

Common questions

I cannot see my journeys

First confirm you are signed into the same account you used when the journeys were created. Refresh the app once. If your collection still does not appear, stop creating new content and contact support so we can investigate without overwriting anything.

A save did not complete

Keep the draft or page open. Check your internet connection, confirm you are signed in, then retry the save. If you receive a cloud-saving warning, contact support and include the approximate time and what you were trying to save.

My photograph will not upload

Use a JPEG, PNG or WebP image. Large uploads may take longer on mobile connections. If an upload fails, keep the original photograph on your device and retry on a stable connection.

Can I add an old journey?

Yes. Use the date the journey or memory actually happened. Memories Unlocked is designed for both new adventures and stories from years ago.

Can I use it on another device?

Sign in with the same account. Cloud-saved journeys and memories are intended to follow your account across supported devices.

Contact support

Memories Unlocked Support

Email support@memoriesunlocked.app. Please do not email passwords, recovery codes, banking details or other secrets.
