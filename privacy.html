PRIVACY

Your stories stay yours.

This notice explains the information Memories Unlocked uses to provide the service and the choices you have about your data.

Last updated: 28 October 2026.

What information the service may hold

  • Account information such as your email address and authentication records.
  • Journeys, dates, places, memory text, clues, legacy notes and other content you choose to save.
  • Photographs and related file information that you choose to upload.
  • Location coordinates you choose to add to a memory, or device location only when you actively grant permission for a location feature.
  • Technical and security information needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service.

How we use it

We use this information to provide your account, save and display your private collection, enable the features you request, protect the service, investigate faults and meet legal obligations. We do not use your private memories to create public profiles or sell your personal information to advertisers.

Account and cloud services

Memories Unlocked uses specialist service providers to operate the product. Supabase provides core database, authentication and private storage services. Vercel is used for public web/app hosting. Map, image or infrastructure providers may receive ordinary technical request information when their services are loaded. These providers process information under their own security and privacy arrangements.

Passwords

Passwords are handled through the authentication service. Memories Unlocked does not ask you to send your password to support, and you should never email a password or recovery code to us.

Device storage

The app may use browser/device storage for session information, resilience or a local copy while the app is working. A local save is not the same as confirmed cloud synchronisation. When preserving important content, remain signed in and check that cloud saving is available.

Photographs

Photographs you upload are associated with your account and are intended to remain private unless you choose to export or share a story. Where edited copies are created, the product is designed to preserve the original separately.

Lawful use and optional permissions

We process information as needed to provide and secure the service and for legitimate operational interests. Where a device permission such as precise location is optional, the feature is requested only when you choose to use it and you can refuse the permission.

Retention and deletion

We keep account content only for as long as needed to provide the service, protect users, resolve security or support issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask for access, correction or deletion of your personal information by contacting support. Some limited records may need to be retained for legal, security or backup-recovery purposes after a deletion request.

Sharing

Your account stays private by default. When you use export or sharing tools, you decide which story content leaves your account. Once you give an exported copy to another person, that copy is outside your private account and you should share it only with people you trust.

International processing

Some technology providers may process data in countries outside the United Kingdom. Where required, appropriate contractual or legal safeguards are used by those providers for international transfers.

Your UK data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or to object to certain processing. You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office. We will not ask you to disclose your password in order to make a privacy request.

Security

We use account authentication, private storage controls and database access rules designed to limit records to the account that owns them. No online service can promise absolute security, so please use a strong unique password and tell us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.
